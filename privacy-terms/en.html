1.2. Personal Data Processed

The Application processes the data listed below:

a) Account and Identity Data
  • First name, last name, and email address, collected during registration/login
  • Password, stored cryptographically on the server and never stored in plain text
b) Personal Information Form Data
This data is collected for the Application’s research and personalization functions. Completing the form is optional; however, some features may require the form to be completed.

c) Education and Exam Data

  • Theme and language preferences, stored locally on the device
  • Anonymous error and performance logs, including device model, OS version, and app version

1.4. Purposes of Data Processing

  • User authentication and account management
  • Providing educational content, including videos and transcripts
  • Saving video watching progress and enabling users to continue from where they left off

Account information, exam answers, and video progress data are securely stored on the server, in a cloud database.

Your personal data is not sold or rented to any advertising company, data broker, or commercial third party.

CyberSage is an educational application intended for school-age students. Users under the age of 18 should use the Application only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.
